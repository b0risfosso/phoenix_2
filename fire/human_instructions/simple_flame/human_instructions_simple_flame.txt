Human Instructions for Creating a Simple Flame Safely

Use a small candle flame as the simplest controlled example.

Materials

- Candle
- Match or lighter
- Stable nonflammable surface
- Metal tray, ceramic plate, or stone surface
- Cup of water nearby
- Clear open space

Safety Setup

Place the candle on a stable, nonflammable surface. Keep paper, cloth, hair, curtains, plastic, alcohol, fuel, and other flammable materials far away. Work in a ventilated area. Do not create a flame near gas, aerosol sprays, chemicals, or dry outdoor brush.

Steps

1. Place the candle upright on the nonflammable surface.
2. Make sure the wick is exposed and not buried in wax.
3. Keep your hands, sleeves, and face away from the wick.
4. Light the match or lighter.
5. Touch the flame briefly to the candle wick.
6. Once the wick catches, move the match or lighter away.
7. Observe the flame without touching it.
8. To extinguish it, blow gently or use a candle snuffer.
9. Confirm the wick is no longer glowing before leaving the area.

What Is Happening

The wick heats up and melts nearby wax. The liquid wax moves up the wick, vaporizes, and reacts with oxygen in the air. That combustion releases heat and light, forming a small flame.

Do Not Do

- Do not use gasoline, alcohol, lighter fluid, or sprays.
- Do not make a large flame.
- Do not hold the candle while lighting it.
- Do not leave the flame unattended.
- Do not place the flame near curtains, paper, clothing, furniture, or electronics.
- Do not let children or pets near the flame.
- Do not try to relight a hot wick immediately after extinguishing it.
