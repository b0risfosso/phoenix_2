Octant Bird Dashboard: AI Flight Pattern Lab

Tune the parameters that determine the bird’s flight pattern, then press Play Round. The bird explores a centered 3D coordinate system through octants, axes, loops, spirals, and target switches.

The dashboard controls the simulation rules. Optional AI autoplay runs longer rounds and tunes the next round from the previous result.
